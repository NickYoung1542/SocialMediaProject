Natural Geographic Animals

Short educational videos about wild animals: how they live, how they hunt and survive, and the surprising facts that make each one remarkable.

How it works

A personal tool researches an animal from public sources, writes a short script, adds narration, footage and captions, and uploads the finished video to the owner's own TikTok account. Every video is labeled as AI-generated.
