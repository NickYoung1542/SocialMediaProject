Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Summary

Natural Geographic Animals is a personal tool that uploads videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It does not collect personal information from visitors or viewers.

2. Information we handle

  • TikTok authorization tokens. When the owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok provides an access token and refresh token. They are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used solely to upload videos.
  • Account identifiers. The tool reads the connected account's username and posting options (for example, available privacy settings) that TikTok returns, only to complete an upload.

The Service does not read, store or analyze other TikTok users' data, and this website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

3. How information is used

Tokens are used only to upload and publish videos that the Service itself creates. They are never used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. Requests are sent only to TikTok's own APIs, and to the content and AI services used to create the videos (which receive the script and search terms, not personal data).

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until deleted. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services, which invalidates the tokens immediately.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.

8. Contact

Questions can be sent through the project's issue tracker.
